# Picture credits

Every picture in this folder is either made for the course (and released with it under CC BY-SA 4.0) or
taken from Wikimedia Commons under the licence stated below. The Commons pictures were resized; nothing
else was changed. `credits.json` holds the same table: `build.js` copies the credit of each picture into the
notes of the slide that shows it, and `textbook/gen_credits.py` writes the list printed in the books.
