{\small
\textit{Computer Architecture: Program First, Formalise Later.}\\
Textbook of the lectures of ARC12, Computer Architecture and Computer
Architecture Laboratory, University of Naples ``Parthenope'', academic year
2026/27.\par
\vspace{1em}

\noindent
The book follows, chapter by chapter, the twenty-five slide decks of the
course: Chapter~$N$ is Lecture~$N$. The decks, the reference programs of the
\vermone project and the tests that assemble and run every listing are kept
together with the sources of this book; the listings printed here are read from
the same files that the tests execute.\par
\vspace{1em}

\noindent
The book is built around \prg, an open educational runtime for \riscv assembly
and C games that targets the ESP32\nobreakdash-C6 microcontroller, the Espressif
QEMU emulator and the \prgqt desktop host. The platform, its source code and
its documentation are distributed under the MIT License by their authors:
Raffaele Montella (academic supervisor and project lead), Ivan Cafiero and
Simone Boscaglia (student contributors), University of Naples ``Parthenope''.
Readers who use \prg in coursework, reports, theses or teaching material are
asked to cite it through the \fname{CITATION.cff} metadata of its repository
\citep{prg32repo}. The companion volume, \emph{Playing with RISC-V}
\citep{prg32textbook}, is the laboratory manual of the platform.\par
\vspace{1em}

\noindent
The reference implementation of \vermone, the course-long project of the book
(the four programs of Appendix~\ref{app:verm1}), was contributed by Giuseppe
Coviello.\par
